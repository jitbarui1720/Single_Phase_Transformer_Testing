# Single_Phase_Transformer_Testing
AIM:
Determination of the equivalent circuit parameters of a single phase transformer.

Objective:
 To obtain the equivalent circuit parameters from Open Circuit and Short Circuit tests.
 To calculate the efficiency as well as regulation of the transformer at given load condition.
 To conduct Sumpner’s test on two identical single phase transformer & to verify the results with OC & SC tests.
 To obtain the B-H curve for a single-phase transformer.
